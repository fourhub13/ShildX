Shildex Privacy Shield

Privacy Policy

Learn how Shildex collects, stores, protects, and manages your personal data and Discord community information.

1. Information We Collect

Shildex collects minimal required data to operate effectively, including:

  • Discord User IDs & Server (Guild) IDs.
  • Channel & Role IDs for server configuration.
  • Command usage telemetry & error logs.

2. Data We NEVER Collect

Your security and privacy are paramount. Shildex will never collect or store:

  • Private chat messages or message history.
  • Personal payment card credentials.
  • IP addresses or off-Discord personal information.

3. How We Use Data

Collected data is strictly utilized to provide bot operations, maintain custom server settings, track moderation infractions, and execute automated security workflows.

4. Data Storage & Security

All database instances are protected with high-tier encryption protocols and restricted access control. We employ modern defensive measures against unauthorized data breaches.

5. Third-Party Sharing

We strictly do not sell, trade, or lease your data to third-party advertisers or data brokers. Data is only processed through trusted infrastructure hosting providers.

6. Data Retention Period

Server configuration data is retained as long as Shildex remains in your guild. Temporary security logs and cache files are automatically purged every 30 to 90 days.

7. Your Data Rights & Deletion

You have the right to request a copy or complete deletion of your stored data at any time. Server owners can request complete guild data wipes via our Support Server.

8. Children's Privacy

Shildex strictly complies with Discord's Terms of Service and COPPA regulations. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 13 years of age.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Significant changes will be announced in our Discord Support Guild or via official bot announcement features.
